N7 Studio

A private publishing tool for N7's own YouTube channels.

N7 Studio is a small tool N7 uses to manage their own YouTube channels, N7_Spectres (gaming) and N7 KinWorks (AI).

N7 makes and edits every video. N7 Studio helps with the packaging: it writes titles, descriptions and tags, and, once N7 has approved them, updates the videos on N7's channels through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service. It only signs in to channels that N7 owns, and nobody else can sign up for it.
